Safety before anything else

People have always looked up at the sky and wanted to be in it. We train you, body and mind, for the physical and mental demands of flying, with time-tested methods and one rule above the rest: safety comes first, because nothing is more valuable than life itself.

Since 1994 Masters has produced professional pilots now flying for airlines and general aviation in the Philippines and in their home countries. Several batches of the Philippine Army Aviation Battalion trained here and are now command pilots in their own service.

Vision and mission

Our vision: to uphold and advance the highest standards of aviation training, and broaden the professional horizons of aviators, in line with the standards set by CAAP.

Our mission

  • Provide safe and effective aviation training in every program.
  • Hold pilot training to the highest professional standards of knowledge and skill.
  • Keep raising our programs to match international flight training requirements.
  • Build professional discipline in an intensive yet friendly learning environment.
  • Strengthen the formal credentials of every student.

Facilities

Ground school runs at two sites. All flying is from the hangar at Plaridel.

Plaridel hangar

Home of the fleet and of our in-house maintenance team, with its own classroom and briefing room. Plaridel Airport opened in 1935, trained fighter pilots in the Second World War, and is recognized as a historical landmark. It is one of the few airports of its class with taxiways reaching both ends of the runway.

Flight simulators

CAAP-certified fixed-wing and helicopter simulators, with single- and twin-engine setups, for instrument training.

Classrooms

At the Pasay office and the hangar. Special classes take one to five students; regular batches take six to fifteen. Ground instructors are licensed; flight instructors are CAAP certified.

Student lodging

Affordable shared quarters and apartments right by Plaridel Airport, for local and foreign students who fly daily and want to skip the drive from Manila. Male and female quarters are separate. A church, market, hospital and mall are nearby.
