From your medical exam to a commercial license

Every step below is set by CAAP. Hours shown are the CAAP minimums; most students fly more.

  1. 1

    Student pilot authority

    Third-class medical certificate and NBI clearance, filed with CAAP.

  2. 2

    Ground school

    Nine subjects, air law to principles of flight, in Pasay or at the hangar.

  3. 3

    First solo

    After the pre-solo exam and at least 8 flying hours. Ends with a bucket of water.

  4. 4

    Private pilot license

    40 hours total, 20 dual and 20 solo, then CAAP written and practical tests.

  5. 5

    Commercial pilot license

    150 hours, a higher-horsepower rating and a CAAP checkride.

  6. 6

    Instrument rating

    Instrument ground school and at least 20 simulator hours.

  7. 7

    Add-on ratings

    Multi-engine on the Piper Aztec, then flight instructor.

Your classroom has a control tower

Plaridel Airport opened in 1935 and trained fighter pilots during the Second World War. Today it is the only airport in Bulacan and is dedicated mainly to flight training. CAAP staffs the tower during operating hours, so you practice radio calls with a staffed tower from your first lessons.

Our hangar sits on the field with a classroom and briefing room, and student lodging is in the immediate vicinity of the airport.

The day you fly alone

Your first solo is the flight every student works toward. When you climb out of the aircraft, your batchmates meet you with a bucket of water in front of the plane you just flew. In our instructors' experience, that new confidence carries students through the rest of their license, and they push on to it sooner.
